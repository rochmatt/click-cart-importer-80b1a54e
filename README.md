# Marketplace Connect

Build a modern, clean, and highly responsive e-commerce storefront frontend. This website will act as a product aggregator where users are redirected to popular marketplaces to complete their purchases.

Design System & Vibe:

Style: Minimalist, trustworthy, and modern (similar to top e-commerce platforms).

Colors: Clean white background (#FFFFFF), light gray for secondary sections (#F9FAFB), and a vibrant primary brand color (like modern blue or teal) for main accents.

Typography: Clean sans-serif font (e.g., Inter or Roboto).

Responsiveness: Must be perfectly optimized for both mobile and desktop (mobile-first approach is preferred).

Key Sections & Components:

Header/Navbar (Sticky): Include a placeholder Logo on the left, a prominent Search Bar in the middle, and standard icons (User Profile, Wishlist) on the right.

Hero Section: A dynamic banner area with a bold headline, a short sub-headline, and a primary "Explore Products" CTA button.

Categories Section: A horizontal, scrollable row of category icons or pills (e.g., Fashion, Electronics, Home & Living, Beauty).

Product Grid (Crucial Section): A grid layout (2 columns on mobile, 4 on desktop).

Product Card Design: Each card needs:

High-quality image placeholder.

Product title (truncated to 2 lines).

Price tag and star rating.

Action Buttons: Instead of a standard "Add to Cart", create a multi-button layout or a dropdown for external links. Include three distinct outline buttons or badges per card: "Shopee" (accent color: Orange), "Tokopedia" (accent color: Green), and "TikTok" (accent color: Black).

Footer: A clean footer containing "About Us", "Contact", and placeholder social media links.

Interactions & Animations:

Add smooth hover effects to the product cards (e.g., a slight lift or soft shadow increase).

Ensure buttons have clear hover states.
